FAILURE-CASE ANALYSIS
Sample 001289 represents a difficult restoration case. The degraded
input already has relatively high structural similarity to the target,
leaving less room for measurable improvement.

Bicubic achieves 23.119561 dB PSNR and 0.754980 SSIM, while the final
TTA result reaches 23.702488 dB PSNR and 0.756310 SSIM.

The modest improvement indicates that the remaining reconstruction
error is concentrated in image details that are difficult to recover
reliably from the degraded input. This case demonstrates that the
model does not produce uniformly large gains on every image.
